Optional Tiny Quotes Pro

Monthly and yearly subscriptions unlock all 15 categories and unlimited custom quotes. The free tier includes up to 5 enabled categories and 5 custom quotes. Favorites, challenges, streaks, and widgets remain free. Existing saved quotes, private custom quotes and preferences are preserved.

Prices are shown in the app before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings, and restore purchases from the You tab.
