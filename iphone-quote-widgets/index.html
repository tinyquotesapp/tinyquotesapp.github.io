How to add quote widgets to your iPhone

An iPhone quote widget puts a short quotation where you already look: beside your Home Screen apps or on your Lock Screen. Motivation: Tiny Quotes selects complete short quotations together with their authors. Longer passages stay in the app, where there is room to read them.

Widgets are free. The app supports iOS 15 or later, but its widgets require iOS 17 or later. Install or update the app, open it once and choose your categories before adding a widget.

Add a Home Screen quote widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps enter edit mode.
  2. Open the widget picker using Edit → Add Widget, or the + button, depending on your iOS version.
  3. Find Motivation: Tiny Quotes. An existing installation may need an app update before its new name appears.
  4. Choose Small or Medium, tap Add Widget, place it where you want and finish editing.

Small is useful for one compact thought. Medium gives a quotation and its author more room. Neither size needs a Pro subscription.

Add a Lock Screen quote widget

  1. Wake and unlock your iPhone, then touch and hold the Lock Screen.
  2. Choose Customize, then Lock Screen.
  3. Tap the date above the clock for the Inline slot, or the widget area below the clock for Rectangular and Circular styles.
  4. Find Motivation: Tiny Quotes, select the style that fits your layout, close the picker and tap Done.

Lock Screen styles have much less space than a Home Screen widget. The app measures the quotation and full author together, rather than choosing a quote by word count alone. The result depends on the widget style and available space.

Why does the Lock Screen show a different quote?

Each widget family has its own pool of complete quotations that can fit. Longer readings remain in the app instead of being cut off. Inline tries your selected categories first; if none fits, it may use a shorter authored quote from another category. Your in-app category choices are not changed by this fallback.

If the widget is missing or has no quote

  • Confirm your iPhone is on iOS 17 or later and the app is updated.
  • Open the app once and enable at least one category.
  • Return to the widget picker and check the app's full new name.
  • After changing settings, allow iOS time to refresh its widget timeline. Widget updates are controlled by the operating system, not an exact real-time schedule.

A widget is not the entire reading library. Open the app to explore 1,000 sourced quotations, read a longer passage, view its source or save it to Favorites. Existing private custom quotes and saved words remain separate from the authored editorial library.
