Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2026  |  Last updated: June 2026  |  Contact: tinyquotesapp@gmail.com

1. About This App

Tiny Quotes ("the app", "we", "our") is a motivational quotes app available on iOS and Android with bundle ID com.tinyspark.daily, designed to provide daily inspiration.

The app is offline-first — your custom quotes, favorites, streaks, notification schedule, and reading history are stored entirely on your device and never leave it. An internet connection is only used for: (a) anonymously syncing onboarding preferences, (b) syncing daily challenge completion events, (c) syncing in-app feedback you choose to send, and (d) validating premium subscription status in a future version. No account sign-in is required from the user.

2. Information We Collect

2a. Onboarding preferences (stored on your device and anonymously synced):

  • Name — the display name you choose (optional)
  • Notification schedule — your preferred wake time and quiet hours
  • Notification frequency — how many quotes per day you want
  • Quote categories — which of the 15 categories you prefer

These preferences are stored on your device and also synced to our secure database (Firebase/Firestore) using anonymous authentication — no email, name, or sign-in is required. An anonymous user ID (UID) is automatically generated by Firebase to associate your preferences. This sync helps us understand aggregate usage patterns and improve the app. Your name and preferences are never linked to any personally identifiable information.

2b. Purchase information (planned; not collected in the current version):

The current version of the app contains no paid features and collects no purchase information. If a premium subscription or one-time upgrade is introduced in a future version, the transaction would be processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, and we would use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. In that case RevenueCat would receive your App Store / Play Store customer identifier, purchase receipt, and your anonymous Firebase UID to link purchase history to your device, so that subscription status could be restored if you reinstall the app. We would not receive or store your payment card details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for details.

2c. Feedback (optional, anonymously synced):

When you voluntarily submit feedback via the in-app feedback form, the following is sent to our Firestore database: the feedback text, the category you selected (General / Bug / Feature), your anonymous Firebase UID, and a timestamp. No name, email, or other personally identifiable information (PII) is attached by us.

Important: Do not include any personally identifiable information — such as your real name, email address, phone number, or postal address — in the feedback text. We are not responsible for any PII you voluntarily enter in the feedback field. Submitting feedback does not create any obligation on our part to implement, respond to, or act on it.

2e. Daily challenge completions (anonymously synced):

When you complete a daily challenge in the app, the following is queued locally and synced to our Firestore database under your anonymous UID: the challenge ID, the date of completion, and a UTC timestamp. No personal identity, name, or email is attached. This data is used solely to track aggregate challenge engagement and improve the app.

2f. Usage data we do NOT collect:

  • We do not use advertising SDKs or sell data to advertisers
  • We do not collect crash reports automatically
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not require or collect your email address or social login
  • Challenge dismiss actions, streak data, favorites, and custom quotes are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to any server.

4. Local Notifications

All push notifications are scheduled entirely on your device using the system's local notification APIs. No data leaves your device to deliver them. No notification content is logged externally.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the app, contact us at tinyquotesapp@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • All locally stored data (favorites, streaks, custom quotes, settings) can be deleted by uninstalling the app.
  • To request deletion of your anonymously synced Firebase data, email tinyquotesapp@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will delete the record associated with your anonymous UID within 30 days, provided we are able to trace the anonymous ID to your request. Since we use anonymous authentication without personal identifiers, we may not be able to locate or delete specific records without sufficient information to identify your anonymous UID.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect any data stored on your device and, in future, any data synced to our servers. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Third-Party Services

The app currently uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — fonts are bundled in the app and do not make network requests at runtime
  • Apple / Google Push Notification APIs — only for locally scheduled notifications; no user data is shared
  • Firebase (Google) — anonymous authentication and Firestore database to store onboarding preferences, optional user feedback, and daily challenge completion events against an anonymous UID. No email or personal identity is required. See Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — planned for use in a future premium tier to manage in-app subscription and purchase entitlements. RevenueCat is not active in the current version of the app. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via an in-app notice or app update. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version.
